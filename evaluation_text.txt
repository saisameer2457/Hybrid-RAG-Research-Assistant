Question 1:
What is the main idea behind the Transformer architecture?

Ground Truth:
The Transformer architecture replaces recurrence and convolutions with attention mechanisms to model sequence relationships.

Question 2:
Why are Transformers more parallelizable than RNNs?

Ground Truth:
Transformers process all tokens simultaneously using self-attention, unlike RNNs which process sequences sequentially.

Question 3:
What mechanism connects the encoder and decoder in Transformers?

Ground Truth:
Attention mechanisms connect the encoder and decoder in the Transformer architecture.

Question 4:
What does BERT stand for?

Ground Truth:
BERT stands for Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers.

Question 5:
What is the key advantage of BERT's bidirectional training?

Ground Truth:
BERT jointly conditions on both left and right context, enabling deep bidirectional language understanding.

Question 6:
How does BERT differ from traditional unidirectional language models?

Ground Truth:
BERT learns bidirectional contextual representations while traditional language models typically process text in a single direction.

Question 7:

What problem does ALBERT attempt to solve?

Ground Truth:
